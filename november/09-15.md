## Decisions

* Agents send traces to MLflow by OTLP only. A synchronous REST export blocked the agent for about 3 minutes while MLflow was down.
* The platform creates the MLflow experiments, not the agent.
* The use case always combines agents with traditional ML.
* ACM DL is dropped from the search.
